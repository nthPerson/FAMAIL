## Headline

1. **Table 6 is still ~10 mm too wide at HEAD.** Commit `8367bf8`'s `\small` +
   `\tabcolsep 4pt` shrank it but did not land it; the commit's "verified by rendering
   page 9 … now inside the column" does not match the committed `main.pdf`, which I
   rendered and which shows the identical overhang. It is now *logged* (29.65 pt
   Overfull), no longer silent.
2. **Three more tables and one equation also overflow the column**, plus ~10 prose
   lines ≥ 8 pt. None of this was noticed because (a) the log is ISO-8859-encoded, so a
   plain `grep Overfull main.log` treats it as binary and returns *nothing* — use
   `grep -a`; and (b) for tables, right-aligned numbers stay flush to the tabular's own
   rules, so the eye has no broken edge to catch unless compared against the caption's
   wrap width.
3. **Systemic cause for the prose overflows:** the house style's hyphenated compounds
   ("counterfactual-perturbation", "transportation-equity", "identity-preservation",
   "attribution-targeted"). TeX breaks an explicit compound only *at* its hyphen and
   refuses to hyphenate inside either half, so a long compound near a line end leaves no
   legal break. Fixes are per-site rewordings, not a global switch.
4. **Microtype caveat:** the local preamble disables font expansion
   (`\microtypesetup{expansion=false}`); Overleaf has it on. Expansion can absorb only a
   few pt of *text* overflow and nothing of table/equation boxes. Treat < ~5 pt items as
   possibly local-only; everything ≥ 10 pt and all box overflows are real everywhere.
