  \caption{Why FATE edits trajectory data: two corpora, two futures.
  A real fleet's service demonstrates bias: on Shenzhen the advantaged
  district receives $3.0\times$ the taxi service per unit demand.
  The recorded trajectories are human-generated and therefore valuable
  --- filtering them would discard useful expertise, so FATE performs
  bounded edits on an attributed slice of the corpus containing the
  most-biased demonstrations (the \emph{edited corpus}, right).
  A policy imitating the raw corpus learns the service gap
  and sends supply to over-served areas; trained
  on the upweighted edited demonstrations, the fairness survives training
  and results in the model learning a fairer policy. The $+$ marks flag
  what the edit changed. Figure~2 shows how the editor works.}
